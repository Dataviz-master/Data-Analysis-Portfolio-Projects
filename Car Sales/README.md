# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 💼 Project Summary

I developed an interactive **Car Sales Dashboard** using Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. This project demonstrates my ability to analyze data, build dashboards, and communicate insights clearly to support decision-making.

---

## 🎯 What I Bring

* Strong skills in **data analysis and storytelling**
* Ability to convert complex datasets into **clear, actionable insights**
* Hands-on experience with **Power BI, DAX, and data modeling**
* Focus on **business impact**, not just visuals

---

## 📊 Key Business Impact

* Identified **$371M+ in YTD sales performance**
* Tracked **monthly trends ($54M MTD)** for real-time monitoring
* Analyzed **13K+ car sales** to uncover patterns in customer preferences
* Highlighted **top-performing brands and regions** to guide strategy

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 🔹 Sales Performance Tracking

* Year-to-Date (YTD) and Month-to-Date (MTD) analysis
* Weekly sales trends to identify growth patterns

### 🔹 Customer & Product Insights

* Breakdown by **Body Style** (SUV, Sedan, etc.)
* Analysis of **Color preferences**

### 🔹 Business Performance Analysis

* Company-wise comparison (e.g., Chevrolet, Ford, Toyota)
* Metrics: Sales, Avg Price, Units Sold, % Contribution

### 🔹 Detailed Data Exploration

* Drill-down into transaction-level data:

  * Customer
  * Dealer
  * Model
  * Sales Value
