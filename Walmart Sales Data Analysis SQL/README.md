🛒 Walmart Sales Data Analysis (SQL Project)
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Walmart sales data using SQL Server. The goal is to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and business insights through structured data analysis. The dataset is cleaned, transformed, and analyzed to extract meaningful insights for decision-making.



📂 Project Structure

The project is organized into separate folders for better clarity and workflow:

Database setup: Contains scripts to create the database environment.
Table creation: Defines the structure of the sales data table.
Data loading: Loads raw CSV data into SQL Server using bulk import.
Analysis: Contains SQL queries used for business insights and reporting.
Dataset: Raw Walmart sales CSV file used for analysis.




📊 Results, Findings & Business Insights

🧾 Revenue Performance by Product Line
Food and Beverages generated the highest revenue (~$56K+), making it the top-performing product line in the dataset.
Sports and Travel also contributed strong revenue (~$55K+), closely following the top category.
Health and Beauty recorded the lowest revenue (~$49K range), making it the weakest performing product line in terms of sales.
Overall, there is a clear revenue gap of around $6K–$8K between top and bottom product lines, showing uneven product demand.





🏙️ Revenue by City and Branch
Naypyitaw (Branch C) generated the highest total revenue (~$110K+), outperforming other branches.
Yangon (Branch A) recorded moderate revenue (~$100K range).
Mandalay (Branch B) showed slightly lower revenue compared to other cities, making it the lowest-performing branch overall.
This shows Branch C is the strongest revenue contributor in the entire dataset.



👥 Customer Type Revenue Contribution
Member customers generated higher total revenue (~$164K+), making them the dominant customer segment.
Normal customers contributed lower revenue (~$158K+), showing a clear but small gap in spending behavior.
This indicates loyalty-based customers (Members) are more valuable in revenue generation.




👤 Gender-Based Revenue Behavior
Female customers contributed slightly higher total revenue (~$167K+) compared to males (~$155K+).
The difference shows that female customers are marginally higher spenders overall.
However, the gap is not extreme, indicating balanced purchasing behavior across genders.




🛍️ Product Line Performance (Sales Volume)
Electronic Accessories and Food & Beverages show the highest transaction volume, meaning they are the most frequently purchased categories.
Health and Beauty has relatively fewer transactions, confirming lower customer demand.
High transaction volume aligns closely with higher revenue categories, confirming strong demand-driven sales structure.




⏰ Time of Day Sales Behavior
Afternoon period generates the highest sales activity, contributing the majority of transactions.
Evening follows closely with strong performance, showing continued demand after peak hours.
Morning has the lowest sales volume, making it the weakest revenue period of the day.
This shows a clear peak demand window between afternoon and evening hours.



⭐ Customer Rating Insights
Average ratings across all product lines remain stable (around ~6.8–7.0 range), showing consistent customer satisfaction.
Food and Beverages and Sports & Travel receive slightly higher ratings, indicating better customer experience in these categories.
No major negative rating outliers were observed, suggesting overall stable service quality across branches.




📌 Overall Business Conclusion
The business is highly driven by a few strong product lines, especially Food & Beverages and Sports & Travel.
Branch C (Naypyitaw) is the strongest revenue-generating location, outperforming other branches.
Member customers and female customers contribute slightly higher revenue, indicating stronger value segments.
Sales activity is heavily concentrated in afternoon and evening hours, showing clear peak business periods.
Overall revenue distribution is uneven but stable, with clear high-performing categories and locations driving growth.
